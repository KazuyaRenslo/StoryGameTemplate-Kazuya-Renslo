Introduction:

Welcome to the society agent. We are an elite group of assassins tasked by the greatest and most powerful people in the world, with fulfilling their requested missions and hits. We have operated in silence and secrecy for 112 years, and our organization was pivotal in both WW1 and WW2, as well as the cold war. We organized the killing of Arch Duke France Ferdinand and have manipulated the balance of power ever since. You have been chosen to join our elite force, by The Eyes, but you must prove yourself worthy of being one of us, so we have given you your first deployment.

Prologue:

Your Mission is simple. You are to kill the president of the United States and help allow the full collapse of the USA. You will arrive at the white house undercover, your identity will be as a Michelin star chef arriving to cook the meals for the delegates from China and America, arriving to have talks with the president. Once inside you will move swiftly and retrieve the Jarin root, left in the janitor's closet by our inside man, you will take any opportunity to hurt the US that you find, and will return to the kitchen. You will then poison the president's food, and evacuate through the rose garden tunnel. You will have 30 minutes from when you enter the kitchen to when you must evacuate and you must not be caught.

Task: Retrieve the Jarin root from the janitor's closet and poison the president's food.
Advanced task: Sabotage the US economy and operations
